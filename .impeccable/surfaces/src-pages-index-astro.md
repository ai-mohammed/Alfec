# Homepage (FR `/`, EN `/en/`)

Scope: the bilingual homepage and, through the shared system, every other page. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: maîtres d'ouvrage, maîtres d'œuvre, general contractors, local authorities, industrial operators.
Job: judge whether ALFEC can carry the technical lots of a building or industrial project. Action: start a
project enquiry (Contact) or call. Proof: the six trades and their real descriptions, ALFEC's own site
photography with captions, 22 wilayas of intervention, named references (Sonelgaz Tiaret, BEA Oran), NAPEC 2018.
Constraints: no invented facts; photography shown no larger than its files support (best authentic file 1521 px).
Memorable moment: the six trades read as one numbered index where each row brings its own installation photo.
Unresolved: final hero photo crop after the first render; whether the 22-wilaya map renders on home or Company only.

## Direction contract

THESIS: ALFEC presented as the serious regional EPC contractor it is: one contractor for six technical trades,
from study to maintenance, with the restraint and finish of Arup, Vinci Energies and Buro Happold. It refuses the
generic contractor template (full-bleed stock hero, icon-box service grid, counters, testimonials) and every
SaaS landing-page device.

OWN-WORLD: White and cool grey grounds; ALFEC navy owns whole fields (trade index, enquiry band, footer); the
logo's sky blue only marks small active details. One grotesque, Archivo, semi-expanded for display and normal
width for text, tabular numerals for 01 to 06. Square corners, 1px rules, no shadows, no gradients, no cards.
Photography is ALFEC's own site documentation, captioned, never stock, never upscaled past its file.

STORY: In one viewport the visitor learns that ALFEC is an Oran-based contractor delivering six complementary
technical trades across Algeria; the trade index, real photographs and named facts make it believable; the
enquiry band and header button make the next step obvious.

FIRST VIEWPORT: 1440 x 900. Top bar: logo left, Entreprise, Métiers, Réalisations, Contact, FR/EN, navy
"Parler de votre projet" button right. Headline across 8 of 12 columns, semi-expanded Archivo about 72px, navy,
two to three lines. Beneath it, left 4 columns: deck naming the six trades, primary action and phone; right 8
columns: the plant-room photograph, about 760px wide, captioned. The trade index begins at the fold.
Signature interaction: hovering or focusing a trade row swaps the index photograph to that trade's installation.
Motion: one entrance for the headline and photo on load, nothing else animates on scroll; static under
reduced motion.

FORM: The category standard, played straight at the craft bar of Arup, Vinci Energies and Buro Happold (the
standing exit, chosen by the user); seed key d09a8cb4.
